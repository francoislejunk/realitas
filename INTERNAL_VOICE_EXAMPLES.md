# Internal Voice Examples

## Overview

The internal voice is how the vessel's memories are relayed diegetically to the user. It represents genuine inner monologue - first-person thoughts that reflect the memory naturally.

## Guidelines

**Good Internal Voice:**
- First person ("I", "my", "me")
- 1-2 sentences
- Matches emotional tone
- Feels like genuine thought
- Natural and conversational

**Bad Internal Voice:**
- Third person ("He", "She", "They")
- Too long or explanatory
- Doesn't match tone
- Feels artificial or forced
- Meta-game or mechanical

## Examples by Availability

### AVAILABLE_NOW (Positive/Accessible)

#### Family - Mother
**Memory:** "You have a loving mother, Margaret, who lives in the suburbs"
**Internal Voice:** "I should call mom soon. She worries when I don't check in."

#### Family - Best Friend
**Memory:** "You have a best friend named Jake who's always had your back"
**Internal Voice:** "Jake would know what to do. I should give him a call."

#### Location - Favorite Diner
**Memory:** "You know a great diner called Sal's Place on 5th Street"
**Internal Voice:** "Sal's always has the best coffee. Good place to think."

#### Possession - Car
**Memory:** "You own a reliable 1987 Honda Civic that's never let you down"
**Internal Voice:** "That old Civic's been with me through everything."

#### Skill - Guitar
**Memory:** "You learned to play guitar from your uncle when you were young"
**Internal Voice:** "I haven't picked up the guitar in months. Maybe I should."

---

### AVAILABLE_LATER (Distant/Strained)

#### Family - Sister
**Memory:** "You have an estranged sister, Sarah, who moved to California years ago"
**Internal Voice:** "I wonder how Sarah's doing. Maybe I'll reach out someday."

#### Relationship - Ex
**Memory:** "You had a serious relationship with someone named Alex, but it ended badly"
**Internal Voice:** "I hope Alex is doing okay. We were good together once."

#### Location - Old Bar
**Memory:** "You used to go to a bar called The Rusty Nail, but it might be closed now"
**Internal Voice:** "The Rusty Nail was my spot. Wonder if it's still around."

#### Possession - Motorcycle
**Memory:** "You had a motorcycle but sold it last year when money was tight"
**Internal Voice:** "I should never have sold that bike. Biggest mistake."

#### Occupation - Former Job
**Memory:** "You used to work as a mechanic but left the shop after a falling out"
**Internal Voice:** "I miss working with my hands. Maybe I'll find another shop."

---

### AVAILABLE_NEVER (Absent/Lost)

#### Family - Father
**Memory:** "You never knew your father. He left before you were born"
**Internal Voice:** "I've been alone for so long. Family is just a word to me."

#### Family - No Siblings
**Memory:** "You're an only child and always wished you had siblings"
**Internal Voice:** "Sometimes I wonder what it would've been like to have a brother."

#### Location - No Home
**Memory:** "You've never had a place to call home, always moving from place to place"
**Internal Voice:** "Home. What does that even mean to someone like me?"

#### Possession - Never Owned Car
**Memory:** "You've never owned a car, always relying on public transport or walking"
**Internal Voice:** "Everyone else has a car. I'm still taking the bus."

#### Relationship - Always Alone
**Memory:** "You've always been a loner, never really connecting with anyone"
**Internal Voice:** "I've learned to be okay with being alone. It's safer that way."

## Emotional Tone Matching

### Warm/Hopeful
- "I should visit mom. She always makes me feel better."
- "Jake would love to hear from me. I'll call him tonight."
- "Sal's Place always feels like home."

### Melancholic/Wistful
- "I wonder how Sarah's doing. Maybe someday we'll talk again."
- "I miss the old days at The Rusty Nail."
- "I should never have sold that bike."

### Resigned/Accepting
- "I've been alone for so long. It's just how it is."
- "Home is just a word to me now."
- "I've learned to be okay with being alone."

### Regretful/Painful
- "I hope Alex is doing okay. I messed that up."
- "Leaving the shop was a mistake. I should've stayed."
- "I wish I'd known my father. Maybe things would be different."

### Neutral/Matter-of-fact
- "That old Civic's been with me through everything."
- "I haven't picked up the guitar in months."
- "The bus works fine. I don't need a car."

## Context-Specific Examples

### Seeing a Happy Family (Triggers Family Memory)

**AVAILABLE_NOW:**
- Memory: "You have a loving family"
- Narration: "You see a happy family having a picnic in the park"
- Internal Voice: "Man, I really miss my mom. I should go see her soon."

**AVAILABLE_LATER:**
- Memory: "You have an estranged family"
- Narration: "You see a happy family having a picnic in the park"
- Internal Voice: "We used to do that. Before everything fell apart."

**AVAILABLE_NEVER:**
- Memory: "You never had a family"
- Narration: "You see a happy family having a picnic in the park"
- Internal Voice: "Must be nice. I wouldn't know what that's like."

### Passing a Diner (Triggers Location Memory)

**AVAILABLE_NOW:**
- Memory: "You know Sal's Diner"
- Narration: "You pass a familiar-looking diner"
- Internal Voice: "Sal's! I could use some coffee right about now."

**AVAILABLE_LATER:**
- Memory: "You used to go to a diner"
- Narration: "You pass a diner that reminds you of somewhere"
- Internal Voice: "Reminds me of that place I used to go. Wonder if it's still open."

**AVAILABLE_NEVER:**
- Memory: "You've never had a regular spot"
- Narration: "You pass a diner full of regulars"
- Internal Voice: "Everyone seems to know each other. I've never had a place like that."

### Hearing Music (Triggers Skill Memory)

**AVAILABLE_NOW:**
- Memory: "You play guitar"
- Narration: "You hear someone playing guitar nearby"
- Internal Voice: "I haven't played in weeks. I should get back to it."

**AVAILABLE_LATER:**
- Memory: "You used to play guitar"
- Narration: "You hear someone playing guitar nearby"
- Internal Voice: "I used to be pretty good. Maybe I should pick it up again."

**AVAILABLE_NEVER:**
- Memory: "You never learned music"
- Narration: "You hear someone playing guitar nearby"
- Internal Voice: "I always wanted to learn. Never had the chance."

## Writing Tips

### DO:
- ✓ Use contractions ("I'm", "don't", "should've")
- ✓ Keep it brief (1-2 sentences max)
- ✓ Match the emotional tone
- ✓ Sound like genuine thought
- ✓ Reference the memory naturally

### DON'T:
- ✗ Explain the memory mechanically
- ✗ Use third person
- ✗ Write long paragraphs
- ✗ Sound artificial or forced
- ✗ Break immersion

### Good Examples:
- "I should call mom. She worries."
- "Jake would know what to do."
- "I miss that old bike."
- "Maybe someday I'll reach out to Sarah."

### Bad Examples:
- "I have a mother named Margaret who lives in the suburbs and worries about me."
- "The character should contact their friend Jake for advice."
- "This memory makes me feel sad about selling my motorcycle."
- "I am experiencing feelings of regret regarding my estranged sister."

**Internal Voice:** "Man, I really miss my mom. I should go see her soon."

The internal voice is the **emotional heart** of the memory system. It transforms mechanical memory creation into **genuine character moments** that feel natural, immersive, and meaningful.

**Good internal voice = Good immersion**
