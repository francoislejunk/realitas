---

## **Two-Part Structure:**

### **Part 1: LLM Contextual Narrative** (2-3 sentences)
- Generated by LLM based on full context
- Describes what happened in the exchange
- Uses correct perspective (You/actor name)
- Matches the polarity (boost vs penalty)

### **Part 2: N2N Formula** (Deterministic)
- Always appended by the system
- Shows exact mechanical outcome
- Format: `"[Winner] [verb] [loser]'s [action], with [affected]'s [STATUS] experiencing [magnitude] [boost/penalty]."`
- Then: `"This causes [affected]'s [STATUS] to go from [original] to [new] with [magnitude] [STATUS] [boost/penalty]."`
